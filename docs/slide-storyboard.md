# Round-one deck: three slides

The guidelines are explicit: three slides excluding cover, no context setting,
sources in an appendix. So no pharma-landscape slide, no "about Cipla", no
methodology preamble. Every slide leads with a conclusion and carries the
evidence underneath it.

Export the underlying tables with `cardiac-agent export`.

---

## Slide 1. The agent, and what it found

**Headline:** *Cipla is growing at a third of the Cardiac market's rate. Two
configurations inside it are winnable, and both sit next to brands Cipla
already owns.*

**Left third: how the agent works.** One diagram, no prose.

```
 7,452 SKUs  ──▶  108 opportunity spaces  ──▶  4-pillar score  ──▶  ranked shortlist
 (6 levels)            ▲                              ▲
                       │                              │
        14 cited external signals ────────────────────┘
                                          ▲
                   every number computed deterministically;
                   the model narrates and is verified against the evidence
```

Three captions only:

- **Inputs.** Cardiac audit to MAT Feb 2026, plus 14 sourced external signals
  covering epidemiology, guidelines, pricing regulation and the innovation
  pipeline.
- **Framework.** Market Opportunity Index rates a space for anybody. Cipla
  Priority Score is that index passed through a right-to-win gate. Separating
  them is what makes the trade-off visible.
- **Guardrail.** The model never calculates. Every figure is checked against
  the evidence pack before the answer is released.

**Right two-thirds: the two-axis chart.** Market opportunity on the vertical,
right to win on the horizontal, bubble size the market value, quadrant lines at
70. Label only six spaces:

| Space | Opportunity | Right to win | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Statin combinations | 77.9 | 74.3 | Double down |
| AHT triple / poly combinations | 79.2 | 67.3 | Build capability |
| Cilnidipine + telmisartan | 80.3 | 60.8 | Build capability |
| Rosuvastatin + ezetimibe | 74.8 | 61.7 | Build capability |
| Other lipid reducers | 66.0 | 42.7 | Avoid or partner |
| AHT diuretic combinations | 45.4 | 48.4 | Harvest |

**The line to say out loud:** the top-right quadrant is where Cipla should
spend. The top-left is a real opportunity that somebody else will win, and the
framework is built to tell those apart rather than average them together.

## Slide 2. Two priorities, and the trade-offs behind them

**Headline:** *Prioritise the ezetimibe extension of Rosulip and the
cilnidipine combination family. Both are brand extensions, not standing starts.*

**Panel A. Statin combinations, entered through ezetimibe.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Sub-segment | ₹4,154 cr, +17.4%, real +10.3% |
| Structure | HHI 1,080, 143 players, leader USV 25.0% |
| Cipla today | ₹57.9 cr, 1.39% share, +15.1% |
| The specific target | Rosuvastatin + ezetimibe: ₹183 cr, **+87.7%**, 12 new entrants last year, no leader above 21% |
| Right to win | ROSULIP is ₹54.3 cr growing 18.1%; ROSULIP EZ already launched |
| Five-year view | ₹703 cr, **18.6 pp ahead of the market** |
| Robustness | Top three in **100%** of 500 randomised weightings |

**Panel B. Cilnidipine combinations, entered through Cresar.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Franchise | ₹2,519 cr across plain and combination packs, +21.5%, real +13.1% |
| Cipla today | **0.19% share**, ₹4.8 cr, growing 40.3% |
| Where to enter | Not the plain molecule (HHI 3,261, Torrent 55.4%). The combinations: dual HHI 1,747, triples HHI 874 to 1,170 |
| Right to win | CRESAR is a ₹51.2 cr telmisartan franchise; CRESAR LN already sells at ₹2.0 cr, +27.9% |
| Five-year view | Cilnidipine + telmisartan reaches ₹1,804 cr, **6.7 pp ahead of the market** |
| Robustness | Top five in **98.8%** of randomised weightings |

**Panel C. How three trade-offs were resolved.** One line each.

- **Size against growth.** AHT dual combinations is larger at ₹5,280 cr, but
  only 4.5% of its 12.6% growth is real. Chose real growth: price gains do not
  compound under an administered ceiling.
- **Growth against competition.** Other lipid reducers grows fastest at 45.6%,
  and is 60% held by one company in an originator molecule. Chose
  accessibility.
- **Attractiveness against right to win.** Cilnidipine + telmisartan scores
  higher and is four times larger; ezetimibe is winnable sooner. Resolved by
  sequencing, not by choosing.

## Slide 3. Where else to play, and where to stop

**Headline:** *Five molecule franchises are underpenetrated and reachable.
Three positions should be harvested, not defended.*

**Panel A. Underpenetrated with a route in.** Penetration index is Cipla's
share of the space against its 1.68% share of the therapy area.

| Franchise | Size | Cipla | Penetration | Gap to fair share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cilnidipine | ₹2,519 cr | 0.19% | 11 | ₹37.4 cr |
| Telmisartan | ₹7,621 cr | 1.19% | 71 | ₹37.0 cr |
| Clopidogrel | ₹1,760 cr | 0.30% | 18 | ₹24.2 cr |
| Acetylsalicylic acid | ₹2,025 cr | 0.65% | 39 | ₹20.7 cr |
| Chlortalidone | ₹1,413 cr | 0.40% | 24 | ₹18.0 cr |

Roughly ₹137 cr of value at fair share. Every one has a Cipla molecule or brand
behind it, which is why they are targets rather than wishes.

**Panel B. Stop funding.**

| Position | Why |
| --- | --- |
| AHT diuretic combinations | Reported +7.2%, but real **-1.0%** and volume **-0.8%**. Cipla declining 14.6% inside it. |
| ACE inhibitors | Real growth **-5.5%**. Structural decline. |
| Anti-angina | ₹1,157 cr and Cipla's share is effectively zero. No adjacency, no fragmentation. |
| Other lipid reducers | Fastest growth in the market, HHI 3,953, one company at 60%. In-license or leave it. |

**Panel C. The implication.** Cipla holds 24.3% of fibrates, its strongest
position anywhere in Cardiac, in a ₹114 cr sub-segment growing 6.4% in real
terms. Its weakest positions are in the fastest-growing spaces. **The portfolio
is optimised for a market that has already moved.** Concentrating on two
configurations beats defending nine.

## For the finale, when the agent is tested

- It answers with no API key configured. The deterministic renderer produces the
  same numbers; only the prose differs. A credential problem cannot stop the
  demonstration.
- Ask it something out of scope. It refuses and explains what it does cover.
- Ask it to justify a recommendation. It returns the pillar-by-pillar score
  decomposition, not an assertion.
- Ask what happens if the weights change. `sensitivity_analysis` answers with a
  number in about four seconds.
- Ask it about saroglitazar. It declines to recommend entry and explains the
  structure, which is the behaviour the competitive-headroom pillar exists to
  produce.
